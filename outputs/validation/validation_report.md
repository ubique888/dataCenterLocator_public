## Counts and filters

- EPA source: **190,976** unique `Cross-Reference Number` site IDs and valid site points. The source's other `Site ID` repeats 27,423 times, so it is retained as a source reference rather than primary key.
- Initial conjunction: reported acreage **≥50 acres**, transmission **≤3 mi**, voltage **≥115 kV**, substation **≤5 mi**. **8,479** pass; missing required inputs fail. All 190,976 remain in upstream enrichment tables; `site_features.parquet` publishes only the 8,479 passing records, **71 columns**, **0 duplicate site IDs**.
- EIA: **2,662** retired plant IDs loaded; **1,754** fully retired plant points with coordinates used for matching; **472/8,479** candidates have a ≤2 km proximity match. Only **14** of those have name corroboration or very close power-site evidence in the evidence-backed frontend former-power filter. The other 458 are retained as nearby EIA records, not verified site identity.
- FRS: **5,329,863** raw rows, **74,649** selected manufacturing facilities, **68,820** with valid points; **5,185** candidates have at least one selected facility in 5 km. CWNS: **17,544** treatment plants, **17,350** usable points; **4,409** candidates have a point within 5 km.
- Local bundle: **77,749** FRS and **6,514** CWNS site–facility pairs within exact WGS84 geodesic 5 km, with **0** per-site count mismatches against the feature table. Its 5 km circle is a display guide. Live HIFLD/FRA lines are selected by ArcGIS 5 km spatial queries; the service may return full segments extending beyond that circle.
- The map's seven controls are a tighter exploratory view, not new rows in the candidate table. Inheritance ≥60 and symbiosis ≥75 yields **2** candidates. The separate dual Top 20 export uses each axis's 75th percentile (849 candidates in the pool); this is a distinct ranking convention, not a blended score.

## Error checks and decisions

1. **Spatial identity and land-use errors:** The 2 km EIA point rule creates plausible but unrelated neighbors. Sunnyvale LF/Yahoo! HQ is the clearest observed example. The legacy score gates proximity-only identity to zero and the UI former-power filter now requires name/very-close evidence. Newtown Creek and Leeton show that a coordinate and high data completeness can still refer to the wrong *kind* of site or an obsolete status. These records remain visible with caveats rather than silently removed.
2. **Extreme scores:** Davis Street reaches 100 symbiosis through dense selected FRS facilities, a close sink and water proxy. Newtown's 99.45 is inflated as a siting lead by its waterbody site-unit problem. Solutia's 98.11 may count a same-complex FRS alias as an external sink. The scoring caps inputs and maintains separate axes, but a high score is only a prioritization signal.
3. **Missing versus zero:** **8,007** passing candidates have no ≤2 km EIA match and their former MW stays `null`, not zero. **1,146** candidates have a WWTP within 5 km but no reported flow; their 5 km flow remains `null`. Exactly **one** candidate has a nearby WWTP with reported zero design flow. No-WWTP candidates have a defined zero 5 km sum and count 0. The detail panel shows `Unknown` for missing design flow and `0.00 MGD` for known zero.
4. **Capacity wording:** Retired generator MW is **historical nameplate** from EIA-860M, never available grid power. CEC figures differ for three California examples, illustrating differing unit sets and source definitions. Some properties have active generation, decommissioning, active waste operations, remediation, or potential restart proposals. None is certified suitable for a data center by these sources.
5. **Outliers and coverage:** Four passing candidates retain source transmission values above 765 kV and are flagged for review; source substation voltage's raw unit is ambiguous and excluded from score. FRS excludes 5,829 selected facilities with unusable coordinates; CWNS excludes 194 approximate/missing points. No external public record proves absence of a nearby user, WWTP, line, or rail merely because the modeled count is zero.

## Verification and remaining limits

The final Parquet, CSVs and HTML are generated by the repository's `scripts/` pipeline; source hashes and stage summaries are preserved in `outputs/validation/`. Local FRS/CWNS pair distances use WGS84 geodesics and were reconciled with all 8,479 scored site counts. Public records corroborated broad site identities for 15 deliberately selected cases and exposed the above material counterexamples. This is a targeted audit, **not** a statistically representative accuracy estimate. The in-app browser blocked opening/testing the local HTML and a direct live ArcGIS query; Leaflet/USGS and live HIFLD/FRA rendering therefore remain unobserved at runtime in this environment. The page exposes load errors/truncation and does not replace field survey, parcel/title, engineering, water-quality, permitting, or commercial diligence.
