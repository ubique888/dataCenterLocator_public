## Speaker-note logic

1. Introduce the seeming contradiction and the inference-specific hypothesis. State that location serves user experience, not just real-estate cost.
2. Explain why urban density cuts both ways. Workforce is strategic context, not a measured score in this project.
3. Explain that the implementation maps nearby assets and potential partners. Distances do not establish service, heat offtake or reuse rights.
4. Walk through the actual pipeline, its source cohort and separate axes. Mention the 50-acre inherited filter and the standalone connectivity preview.
5. Compare two candidates without calling either viable. The chart screens for diligence and avoids a blended score.
6. Recommend Astoria/Queens as a region to investigate first, not a build-ready parcel. Name the decisive missing checks.
7. Explain the user's resident-demand and suburban-land premises, plus durable connections and adaptable hardware/routing. Label these as planning premises and describe the conditional 2030–2050 vision without claiming forecast benefits.
